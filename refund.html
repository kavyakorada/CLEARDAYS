Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your ClearDays plan, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] within 7 days of purchase with your registered email address and payment ID. We will give you a full refund. No questions asked.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed within 2 business days of your request and credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

Cancellation

All plans are one-time payments with no auto-renewal, so there is nothing to cancel. You will never be charged again unless you choose to buy another plan.

After 7 days

Requests made more than 7 days after purchase are not eligible for a refund, except where a payment was made twice by mistake or the plan could not be delivered. In those cases we always refund in full.
