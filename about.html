About us

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ClearDays is a digital self-care programme that helps adults with migraine change what is on their plate. Over 28 days, it gently moves people away from excess sugar, white rice, oil, tamarind and salt and towards millets, fruits, vegetables, sprouts and herbs, inspired by naturopathy. It also covers regular meals, water, sleep, calm practices and a simple daily diary to help people learn their personal triggers.

What ClearDays is not

ClearDays is food and lifestyle education. It does not diagnose, treat or cure migraine or any other medical condition, and it is not a substitute for a doctor. Please keep following your doctor's advice and never stop prescribed medicine on your own.
